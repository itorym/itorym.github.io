ABOUT

Biography

I received the B.E. degree from the National Defense Academy of Japan in 2009, the M.S. degree from the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology in 2015, and the Ph.D. degree in engineering from Osaka University in 2019.

From 2009 to 2020, I served in Japan Air Self-Defense Force, Ministry of Defense, Japan. I joined the National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT), Japan, as a Senior Researcher in 2020, and is currently a Planning Manager at the Strategic Planning Office and a Research Manager at the Security Fundamentals Laboratory.

My research interests include the cryptanalysis and design of symmetric key ciphers and security analysis of real-world systems, particularly end-to-end encryption technologies used in messaging applications and video conferencing systems.
